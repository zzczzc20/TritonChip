Where the link clock is set and what changes for 8.0 / 8.5 ns (the RTL carries no clock number)
  - TNCA_KERNEL_MHZ in the environment of the link: flow/fpga/link.sh passes `--kernel_frequency 0:<MHz>` to v++ and checks the xclbin metadata; flow/fpga/package_xo.tcl declares ap_clk FREQ_HZ from the same
    variable (the dry runs below: TNCA_PKG_AP_CLK_HZ=125000000). The value must be a whole number of MHz (10..650): 8.0 ns = 125, 8.5 ns = 118 (8.475 ns) or 117 (8.547 ns).
  - flow/fpga/ooc.xdc line 45 (`create_clock -period 10.000 -name ap_clk`) and flow/fpga/synth_r40_multicore.tcl line 19 (`create_clock -name ap_clk -period 10`) are the constraints of the OOC kernel
    screens, to be 8.000 / 8.5 for a kernel-level screen at the target; the v++ link derives its constraint from the platform clock wizard (the kernel_frequency request).
  - placement strategy of the run-3/run-2 recipe: LINK_EXTRA='--vivado.prop run.impl_1.STEPS.PLACE_DESIGN.ARGS.DIRECTIVE=SSI_SpreadSLLs' (flow/fpga/run_r40_attempt01.sh); further directives (post-route phys_opt etc.) are
    additional --vivado.prop entries in LINK_EXTRA (the flow-directive screen of the c383 tile tests which help).
  - What the census says must change in the RTL before either target: evidence/r42/clk/IMAGE_CENSUS.md (reset tree, HBM outlet adapter enables, dispatch counters, TMU, mesh, bit 7, bit 9).
