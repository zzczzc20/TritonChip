# Physical cost of each lever (R42 dse): recorded against derived

Domain of the numbers: **recorded** = read from a file of the repository (path given), a routed image, a unit or tile screen
(out-of-context place and route at 10 ns), or an RTL parameter; **derived** = arithmetic on recorded numbers by the rule written
next to it; **not recorded** = no screen or synthesis result exists for it, so no number is given. Nothing here was synthesised by
this study; no new link, screen or board run was made.

Anchor facts (recorded):
- One TNCA tile (core + NUCA home slice + its NI) is 246,868 / 247,348 / 246,458 routed LUTs in the run-1 image, 171 DSP, 32 RAMB36
  (`evidence/r41/hbm_bw/RESOURCES.md` 2.1); the whole design is 927,245 LUTs and 524 DSP of the device's 1,303,680 LUTs and 9,024 DSP
  (`RESOURCES.md` 2.1, `STATUS.md` through `doc/r42_arch_headroom.md` 1.3). Each tile sits in its own SLR; all three HBM outlets and the HBM
  subsystem are in SLR0.
- CLB packing of the routed run-2 link: SLR0 95.56 %, SLR1 90.66 %, SLR2 88.06 % (`doc/r42_arch_headroom.md` 1.4 from
  `link_1f0a5aff/utilization_slr.rpt.gz`); the highest packing that ever routed is 96.74 % (run 1). Headroom to it, derived there:
  SLR0 about 4k, SLR1 22k, SLR2 28k LUTs.
- Kernel clock group of the run-3 image: setup WNS +0.111 ns at 10 ns; worst paths: hybrid LSU `fast/rr_reg` to `out_vector` (24 to 25
  logic levels, 75 to 78 % routing), a high-fan-out enable into `u_vfpu` (one LUT, 97.7 % routing), matrix operand capture F09 (fixed by
  `MTX_VRF_REG`) (`doc/r42_arch_headroom.md` 1.6).

| cores 3 -> 4 / 6 / 8 | one more tile (core, NUCA slice, NI), one more HBM outlet and mesh node per core | tile 247k LUT, 171 DSP, 32 RAMB36; outlet 7.4k LUT (1.5k with `AXI_LINE_LUTRAM`, recorded screen); SLR capacity 434,560 LUT | a second tile in an SLR is 494k LUT > 434k; 4 / 6 / 8 cores at today's tile size need 1.17M / 1.67M / 2.16M LUTs of 1.30M.
