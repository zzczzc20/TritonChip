# Descriptor E4M3 K32: tails and two-role experiments (unmerged)

This extends the earlier [aligned experiment](k32_descriptor_pending_20261031.md); its incremental [patch](k32_descriptor_tail_roles_20261031.patch.gz) applies after the earlier candidate patch, not directly to the integration branch. The [raw archive](k32_descriptor_tail_roles_20261031.tgz) retains scripts, logs and results, including failed attempts. Neither patch is merged.

Single-role DP TLM tests of 16x16 descriptor GEMM at K=16, 24, 32, 33 and 64 returned 0/256 bit mismatches for each tested finite, signed-zero and NaN input. The K=33 reference first failed because the oracle requires K divisible by 4; the subsequent reference pads to a four-element group. Cold finite-input visible cycles increased for K=16 (2671 to 2783), K=24 (2921 to 2988) and K=33 (3263 to 3399); K=33 instructions/MAC decreased from 0.0137311 to 0.0123106. Each used one launch and no code reload during the launch. The intermediate v4 default-code audit compared 113/113 unchanged; the final v6 audit is pending.

The first two-role attempt (v5) failed all 12 cases because the test supplied role mask 2, activating only one role; its failures are retained and are not counted as candidate passes. After correcting the harness role mask to 3, all 12 v6 combinations of K=32/64/96, rolled/unrolled and K16/K32 returned 0/256 mismatches, one launch and no reload during launch. Cold visible cycles increased in every K32 comparison: for K=32 unrolled 3398 to 3588, rolled 3420 to 3610; K=64 unrolled 3990 to 4164, rolled 4019 to 4186; K=96 unrolled 4694 to 4894, rolled 4721 to 5261. Instructions/MAC decreased respectively from 0.02490234 to 0.02062988, 0.02636719 to 0.02319336, 0.01708984 to 0.01245117, 0.01794434 to 0.01318359, 0.01448568 to 0.009847005, and 0.01489258 to 0.0115153.

These are uncalibrated TLM visible cycles, not board measurements. The negative cold-cycle results, remaining shape and model coverage, and pending final audit do not justify making this a default or asserting universal K32 support or a speedup.
